Jackass of the Week: John Gruber

John Gruber, reacting to a Financial Times report that Bernie Sanders and Steve Bannon appeared at the same event to call for stronger AI guardrails:

“Another take is the horseshoe theory, which holds that leftwing and rightwing zealots are more alike than different...”

Yes, that is another take. It is also what happens when the political label-maker runs out of tape before the argument starts.

Gruber’s post offers one charitable sentence, then reaches for “horseshoe theory” as though ideological geometry were a substitute for risk analysis. Sanders’s argument is not made foolish because Bannon agrees with part of it. If a smoke alarm goes off and a villain also smells smoke, the grown-up response is to check the building, not accuse the alarm of bipartisanship.
